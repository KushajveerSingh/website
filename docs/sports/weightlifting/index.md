## Plan - Morning (Bodybuilding)

-   4 day full body workout with dumbbell and bench.
-   Schedule
    - Monday - Workout A
    - Tuesday - Workout B
    - Wednesday - Rest
    - Thursday - Workout A
    - Friday - Workout B
    - Saturday - Rest
    - Sunday - Rest
-   Time efficiency
    - Chest + Back
    - Leg + Shoulder

### Warmup

- Push up
- Body weight squat

### Workout A

- Dumbbell bench press 3x 8-15 (chest)
- Pullovers 3x 8-15 (back)
- Sitting or standing overhead press 3x 8-15 (shoulder)
- Bicep curl 3x 15-20 (biceps)
- Skull crusher or overhead extension 3x 15-20 (tricep)
- Rear delt files 3x 15-20 (shoulder)
- Lateral raises 3x 15-20 (shoulder)

### Workout B

- Overhead press 3x 8-15 (shoulder)
- Dumbbell row 3x 8-15 (back)
- Incline bench press 3x 8-15 (chest)
- Hammer curl 3x 12-20 (bicep)
- Tricep kickback 3x 15-20 (tricep)
- Rear delt files 3x 15-20 (shoulder)
- Lateral raises 3x 15-20 (shoulder)
